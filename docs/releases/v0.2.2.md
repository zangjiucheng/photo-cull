# v0.2.2 — A RAW base with more depth, and an Auto fix

## Image quality

- DJI DNGs are now developed through the ProfileToneCurve embedded in the file (the same curve used by the in-camera JPEG and by Lightroom). A +0.6 EV lift is applied before the curve, and hue is preserved. The zero point no longer looks flat and gray; contrast and brightness are close to the in-camera JPEG, and colors are richer.
- Both preview and export change. Saved edit recipes are also shown on the new base and will look brighter with more contrast overall, so they may need some re-tuning.
- RAW files whose DNG has no tone curve develop exactly as before.

## Auto

- Fixed adjustments piling up when Auto was clicked repeatedly: clicking again now replaces the previous result instead of adding to it.
- Adapted to the new base: shadows are no longer lifted by default and are only touched when the darks are crushed or highlights are about to clip; Brilliance is reduced and Vibrance slightly increased, so contrast is not flattened.
