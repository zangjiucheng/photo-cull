# v0.3.0 — New black-and-white interface, Tone/Color pad, and faster processing

## Interface

- The whole app now uses one flat black-and-white style: a pure black background, hairline separators, and white as the only accent color. Only star ratings (yellow) and delete (red) keep functional colors. Cards nested in cards, gradients, and decorative shadows are gone.
- The launcher, Browse toolbar, thumbnails, Review, Similarity, Inspector, editor, Stats, Debug, and the shortcuts panel have all been redone; features and shortcuts are unchanged.
- The Browse sidebar no longer shows a second copy of the thumbnails; the Inspector is now a key-value list; in the editor, Export is the only primary button.

## Editing

- New Apple-style Tone/Color 2D pad (vertical axis More Tone ↔ Softer, horizontal axis Muted ↔ Vivid). As you drag, the Contrast, Black point, Shadows, Highlights, Brilliance, Vibrance, and Saturation sliders move together.
- The direction each photo moves in is decided by its histogram and by skin/sky detection: shadows that are already very deep are not pushed further down, highlights close to clipping are protected first, and skies and portraits get less saturation.
- After dragging you can fine-tune any slider directly, and dragging the pad again keeps your fine-tuning. Double-click to reset; arrow keys nudge the pad.

## Performance

The output pixels of all of the optimizations below are identical to before.

- Color adjustment, the tone curve, and sRGB encoding/decoding are split into row bands and computed in parallel on multiple threads; resizing processes the three channels in parallel.
- The editor caches the downscaled base image: switching back to a photo you already opened drops from about 2.7 s to about 0.01 s, Auto and pad analysis drop to tens of milliseconds, and preview rendering drops from 0.7 s to 0.09 s.
- The previous and next photos are prefetched in the background, so stepping through photos in order rarely waits.
- Export uses its own cache, so it does not evict the photo you are editing and the editor does not stall during export; the next photo is decoded ahead of time. Batch-exporting 3 full-size JPEGs drops from about 12–13.6 s to about 3.5–4.7 s.

## Notes

- The DNG tone curve base added in v0.2.2 applies only to new (v2) recipes. v1 recipes saved before v0.2.0 still use the legacy engine and show "Legacy edit preserved" when opened; click "Use new controls" to switch to the new base (this restarts the adjustments from a neutral state; the old adjustments can be recovered with undo).
